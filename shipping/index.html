Shipping & Logistics

Confirm packing, routing and shipment documents as part of your quotation.

Home › Shipping & Logistics

Define the destination

Provide the destination country, port or delivery address and requested trade terms. Freight and delivery responsibilities need confirmation in the quotation.

Mixed-order packing

Request a line-by-line packing plan for mixed parts. Confirm whether consolidation is available for the specific order and which charges are included.

Size and weight

For bulky body parts and dense components, request packed dimensions, gross weight and the freight calculation basis. Illustrations are not reliable packing measurements.

Documents

Confirm the commercial invoice, packing list, origin documents and any product-specific import requirements with the supplier and your importer before shipment. Document availability must be checked for the actual order.

Lead time and routing

Ask for the proposed departure point, route and estimated schedule. Do not treat an indicative shipping estimate as a guaranteed arrival date.

Need a quotation for Chinese-brand parts?

Send the OE number, or send the VIN and a photo. We confirm applicability and quote with specification, MOQ, lead time and freight to your port.
