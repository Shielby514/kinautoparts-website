Contacting KINP

KINP is the trading brand used on this website by Guangzhou Qinpai Auto Parts Co., Ltd. (广州秦派汽车配件有限公司). For questions about enquiry information, email Ku@kingmay-autoparts.com.

Information in your enquiry

To request a quotation, you may choose to provide your business contact details, OE numbers, vehicle model or VIN, part photographs, quantities and delivery destination. Send only information needed to identify the part and discuss your order. Do not send identity documents or payment-card details with a parts enquiry.

WhatsApp and email links

This website provides links to WhatsApp and your email application. Product-specific links can include the public OE number in a draft message. Opening a draft does not submit an order; review the message and choose whether to send it. WhatsApp and your email provider process messages under their own policies.

Following up on your message

The export team uses information you send to discuss part identification, fitment, availability, quotations and delivery arrangements. To ask about information already sent, contact the team using the email above and identify the relevant enquiry.

Need a quotation for Chinese-brand parts?

Send the OE number, or send the VIN and a photo. We confirm applicability and quote with specification, MOQ, lead time and freight to your port.
