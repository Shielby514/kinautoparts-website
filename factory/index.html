Sourcing & Supply Information

Information to confirm when sourcing Chinese-brand vehicle parts through KINP.

Home › Sourcing & Supply Information

Start with the required part

Send the OE reference, vehicle details, quantity and destination. A catalogue listing is a starting point for an enquiry, not evidence of current stock or factory ownership.

Supplier and product documents

Before an order is agreed, request the manufacturer identity, part specification and any certificates relevant to that specific product. Documents and claimed capabilities require individual verification.

Mixed-parts enquiries

For a mixed order, list each reference and quantity separately. Ask for the packing plan, available quantities and freight arrangement in the quotation.

Availability and lead time

Stock status, production requirements and estimated lead time must be confirmed in the written quotation. No universal dispatch window applies to the catalogue.

Need a quotation for Chinese-brand parts?

Send the OE number, or send the VIN and a photo. We confirm applicability and quote with specification, MOQ, lead time and freight to your port.
