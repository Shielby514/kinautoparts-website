About KINP Chinese Vehicle Parts

A Guangzhou-based wholesale exporter built around one problem: identifying Chinese-brand part numbers outside China.

Guangzhou Qinpai Auto Parts Co., Ltd.

KINP is the automotive parts brand of Guangzhou Qinpai Auto Parts Co., Ltd., based in Guangzhou, China. Through kinautoparts.com, we focus on parts identification and sourcing enquiries for Chinese-brand vehicles, serving importers, distributors, workshops and fleet buyers.
