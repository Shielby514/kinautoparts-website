Who operates KINP?

KINP is the automotive parts brand presented by Guangzhou Qinpai Auto Parts Co., Ltd. (广州秦派汽车配件有限公司). This website focuses on replacement-parts enquiries for Chinese-brand vehicles. The company has submitted a trademark application for KINP; this statement does not claim that registration has been granted.

What does this website help buyers do?

Importers, distributors and workshops can browse brands, models and catalogue references, then request a quotation with an OE number, VIN, quantity and destination. Catalogue entries are identification leads. Fitment, available supply and commercial terms require confirmation for the individual enquiry.

How are quotations confirmed?

Send the complete reference and available vehicle information. The export team reviews the requested application and proposed item, then confirms the quotation details. MOQ, lead time, warranty, packing and freight terms are stated in the quotation rather than promised uniformly across the catalogue.

How do the related websites differ?

KINP focuses on Chinese-brand vehicle parts. KINGMAY Auto Parts serves the Korean-vehicle parts programme. Globalucar covers vehicles and automotive-parts export information; its wholesale channel supports consolidated procurement enquiries. Each website identifies its operating business and enquiry scope; this site does not imply a single legal entity for every related business.
