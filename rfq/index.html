What should a quotation request include?

Send the OE or part number, vehicle brand and model, model year, VIN if available, quantity, destination country or port, and clear photographs of the removed part and markings. If the reference or fitment is uncertain, identify that uncertainty in the request.

How is a mixed order handled?

Send one line per reference, with quantity and supporting evidence. Keep your buyer line IDs consistent across the list and photographs so each proposed supply item can be checked separately.

What will the quotation confirm?

The proposed item, confirmed application where established, quantity basis, MOQ, available supply or production lead time, packing, payment, warranty and delivery terms must be agreed in the written quotation. A catalogue page alone is not a stock or compatibility guarantee.

What happens when information is missing?

The team requests the missing identification details before confirming the proposed item. Do not assume two similar numbers or photographs prove interchangeability.

Need a quotation for Chinese-brand parts?

Send the OE number, or send the VIN and a photo. We confirm applicability and quote with specification, MOQ, lead time and freight to your port.
