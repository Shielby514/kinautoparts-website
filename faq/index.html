Frequently Asked Questions

Answers grouped by topic for distributors and workshops sourcing Chinese-brand auto parts from KINP.

What does KINP specialize in?

KINP focuses on Chinese-brand vehicle parts identification and sourcing enquiries. Its operating company is Guangzhou Qinpai Auto Parts Co., Ltd.

What should a parts RFQ include?

Provide the complete OE reference, any suffix or version, vehicle details where available, quantity and destination. Include clear photographs if identification is uncertain.

Does an OE reference confirm fitment or stock?

No. A catalogue reference is an identification lead. Fitment, product specification and availability need confirmation for the proposed order.

What are the MOQ, lead time and warranty?

These depend on the proposed item and order. Request the applicable quantity, lead time, packing, freight and after-sales terms in the quotation.

Need a quotation for Chinese-brand parts?

Send the OE number, or send the VIN and a photo. We confirm applicability and quote with specification, MOQ, lead time and freight to your port.
