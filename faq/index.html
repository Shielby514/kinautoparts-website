Frequently Asked Questions

Answers grouped by topic for distributors and workshops sourcing Chinese-brand auto parts from KINP.

KINP supplies Chinese-brand auto parts wholesale to importers, distributors, workshop chains and fleet operators. The answers below cover common procurement questions, grouped by topic; each answer carries a permanent anchor so it can be linked to directly.

On this page: Ordering and minimum quantities · Identifying the right part · Quality, sourcing and warranty · Payment and shipping · Brands, markets and who we sell to

Ordering and minimum quantities

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ and quantity units are confirmed for each line in the quotation. Send the complete reference and required quantity; the catalogue does not establish a universal minimum.

Can I mix brands and categories in one order?

Send a mixed-parts list for review. Availability of consolidation, packing and freight arrangements is confirmed for the proposed order; it is not established by a catalogue listing.

How do I request a quotation?

Provide the OE reference or part description, vehicle and model year, quantity and destination. Use the RFQ page to prepare a WhatsApp or email draft, then review and send it in that application.

Identifying the right part

Can you identify a part from the VIN alone?

A VIN can support identification but does not guarantee that every reference can be resolved. Include the part label, vehicle variant and relevant photographs. Unresolved references need further evidence before ordering.

What if I only have a photo and measurements?

Clear photographs can support a parts enquiry. Include labels, connectors, mounting points and relevant measurements; visual similarity alone does not prove fitment.

Do you provide OE cross-references?

Related numbers in a catalogue are reference information. A proposed replacement or superseded number requires its own evidence and application review; numbers listed together are not automatically interchangeable.

Quality, sourcing and warranty

Do you sell genuine parts or aftermarket?

Ask for the proposed manufacturer, product description, packaging and supporting documents. An OE number in this catalogue is an identification reference, not a claim that the item is a genuine vehicle-manufacturer part.

What warranty applies?

Warranty scope, duration, exclusions and remedies must be agreed in the written order terms. No universal warranty period or automatic credit or replacement is promised by this website.

How do you control quality before shipment?

State the inspection, sampling or photograph requirements in the enquiry. The available inspection scope, evidence, responsibilities and any charges need written confirmation before ordering.

Payment and shipping

What payment terms do you work on?

Payment method, currency, timing and any deposit are confirmed in the written quotation and order documents. This website does not accept payments or confirm an order.

Which incoterms and shipping modes do you support?

Specify your requested trade term and named destination. Confirm the applicable term, responsibilities and charges in the quotation before approving the order.

How are bulky body parts freighted?

Ask for packed dimensions, gross weight and the freight calculation basis for bulky items. The applicable charge depends on the shipment and carrier; it cannot be inferred from the product image.

Brands, markets and who we sell to

Which Chinese brands do you cover?

The catalogue organises references for Chinese vehicle brands. Browse the brand and model pages for listed references; inclusion does not confirm current availability or every variant of that vehicle.

Which markets do you ship to?

Include the destination country and port in your enquiry. Routing, documentation and any import requirements need review for the actual product and destination; no local office or local stock is implied.

Do you sell to end consumers?

KINP is positioned for business enquiries from importers, distributors, workshops and fleets. Send the part list and quantity requirements for review; the website is not a retail checkout.

Need a quotation for Chinese-brand parts?

Send the OE number, or send the VIN and a photo. We confirm applicability and quote with specification, MOQ, lead time and freight to your port.
