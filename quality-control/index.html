Quality & Inspection Enquiries

Specify inspection needs and review the evidence required for your parts order.

Home › Quality & Inspection Enquiries

Define the specification

Ask for the identifying dimensions, position, connector details or other attributes relevant to your part. An OE reference alone does not confirm all vehicle variants.

Agree inspection requirements

Include any inspection, sampling, test-report or pre-shipment photograph requirements in the enquiry. Scope, availability and any costs need written agreement before ordering.

Packaging requirements

Specify protection, carton labels and any pallet or crate requirements. Confirm the packing method and responsibility in the quotation.

Report a discrepancy

Keep the quotation, part labels, packing records and clear photographs when reporting a discrepancy. Warranty, returns and remedies depend on the written terms agreed for that order; this page does not promise automatic credit or replacement.

Need a quotation for Chinese-brand parts?

Send the OE number, or send the VIN and a photo. We confirm applicability and quote with specification, MOQ, lead time and freight to your port.
