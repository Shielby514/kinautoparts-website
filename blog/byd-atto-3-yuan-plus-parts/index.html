Published 11 November 2026 · KINP editorial team · Procurement information

Use the vehicle label as the starting point

When an enquiry uses either Atto 3 or Yuan Plus, retain the buyer’s original model name and request the vehicle identification details. A catalogue naming association is not proof that every part is shared across markets or production dates.

Compare the actual part details

Record the OE reference shown on the original part together with connector, mounting and relevant dimensional details. Do not approve an alternative merely because a listing carries both vehicle names.

Separate identification from supply

First establish the required reference and variant; then request quantity, packing and delivery terms. If the identification evidence conflicts, keep the line pending rather than treating a similarly named listing as a confirmed match.

Prepare your quotation request

List the reference, vehicle variant, quantity and destination. Attach identifying photographs in your email or WhatsApp message. Confirm specification, lead time, packing, warranty and delivery terms in writing.
