OE-based identification and enquiry checklist for Chinese EV. Confirm vehicle variant, part details and quotation terms before ordering.

Home › Blog › Chinese EV

For a Chinese EV parts enquiry, start with the complete OE reference and vehicle details. KINP uses catalogue references to organise sourcing enquiries; application and availability require confirmation for the proposed order.

Published 11 November 2026 · KINP editorial team · Procurement information

Identify the system before building the order

Separate the requested component by system, such as braking, suspension, body or cooling. Describe what is being purchased rather than assuming all EV components share a single specification.

Ask for variant-specific evidence

Include model year, powertrain variant, original label and relevant connectors or mounting details. A visually similar image is not enough to establish compatibility, especially for components with electrical or thermal interfaces.

Keep safety-critical work within qualified review

This is a procurement checklist, not a high-voltage diagnostic or installation procedure. Identification and technical approval for safety-critical parts require suitable manufacturer information and qualified personnel.

Confirm commercial terms separately

A catalogue entry does not establish stock, battery compatibility, certification, warranty or transport eligibility. Request the documents and order terms relevant to the actual product and destination.

Prepare your quotation request

List the reference, vehicle variant, quantity and destination. Attach identifying photographs in your email or WhatsApp message. Confirm specification, lead time, packing, warranty and delivery terms in writing.
