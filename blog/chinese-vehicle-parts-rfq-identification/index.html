Chinese Vehicle Parts RFQ Checklist by Brand and OE Reference

Published 12 November 2026 · KINP

For a Chinese-brand parts quotation, send the vehicle brand and model variant, the complete part reference, required quantity and destination. Add a clear part-label photograph when identification is uncertain. A catalogue result starts the enquiry; it does not confirm fitment or available stock.

Start with the vehicle brand

Choose the correct brand in the catalogue, then the model group. Model names may differ between markets, so include the production year and available vehicle identification details rather than relying on the sales name alone.

Keep the complete reference

Copy the full reference from the label or purchasing record, including letters, suffixes and any version or size segment. If two references are listed for one requested item, keep both and ask for identification review. Do not treat them as interchangeable without confirmation.

Prepare a clear purchasing list

Use separate columns for the supplied reference, description, quantity and unit. Put vehicle details and photographs beside the relevant line. Keep alternatives in a separate field rather than replacing the requested reference.

What is confirmed in the quotation?

Ask for the proposed item, identification basis, quantity, packing, availability, lead time and freight terms. These are order-specific details. Catalogue entries alone are not stock or delivery commitments.

Can a brand logo confirm supplier authorization?

No. A logo identifies the vehicle-brand category. Its appearance in the catalogue does not establish manufacturer appointment or affiliation.
