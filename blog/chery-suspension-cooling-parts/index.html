Chery Suspension and Cooling Parts: An RFQ Checklist

OE-based identification and enquiry checklist for Chery suspension and cooling. Confirm vehicle variant, part details and quotation terms before ordering.

Home › Blog › Chery suspension and cooling

For a Chery suspension and cooling parts enquiry, start with the complete OE reference and vehicle details. KINP uses catalogue references to organise sourcing enquiries; application and availability require confirmation for the proposed order.

Published 11 November 2026 · KINP editorial team · Procurement information

Specify the position and variant

For suspension enquiries, record the side and axle position as well as the original reference and vehicle details. Similar-looking components can have different mounting or dimensional requirements.

Describe the cooling component clearly

Identify the component being requested and provide its label, interfaces and relevant dimensions. Do not infer suitability from a generic model-name listing or photograph.

Review alternatives before approving the order

Ask the supplier to explain any proposed reference change and its evidence. Keep unconfirmed replacements separate from the accepted order list. Failure frequency, material grade and service life cannot be inferred from this catalogue.

Prepare your quotation request

List the reference, vehicle variant, quantity and destination. Attach identifying photographs in your email or WhatsApp message. Confirm specification, lead time, packing, warranty and delivery terms in writing.
