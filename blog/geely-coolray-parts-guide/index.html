Geely Coolray Parts: Prepare an OE-Based Enquiry

OE-based identification and enquiry checklist for Geely Coolray. Confirm vehicle variant, part details and quotation terms before ordering.

Home › Blog › Geely Coolray

For a Geely Coolray parts enquiry, start with the complete OE reference and vehicle details. KINP uses catalogue references to organise sourcing enquiries; application and availability require confirmation for the proposed order.

Published 11 November 2026 · KINP editorial team · Procurement information

Keep market and year information together

Include the model name used on the vehicle documents, production year and engine or powertrain details. Market names and catalogue groupings do not independently prove compatibility.

Check the number and the physical interface

Copy the complete original reference, retaining leading zeros and suffixes. Supply photographs of the part label and relevant mounting or electrical interfaces. A partial number can conceal a variant difference.

Request a line-by-line response

For each requested item, ask whether the reference is confirmed, whether an alternative is proposed and what further evidence is required. An alternative needs its own documented review; it is not automatically interchangeable.

Prepare your quotation request

List the reference, vehicle variant, quantity and destination. Attach identifying photographs in your email or WhatsApp message. Confirm specification, lead time, packing, warranty and delivery terms in writing.
