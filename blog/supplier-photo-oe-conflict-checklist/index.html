When Supplier Photos and OE Numbers Disagree: A Chinese-Vehicle Parts Checklist

Published 22 November 2026 · Author: Shirley | Senior Autoparts Procurement Specialist

If an enquiry reference, a packaging label and a supplied photograph disagree, keep the line unresolved. Retain the original complete number, identify the specific conflict and request a readable identification view tied to the same item. Do not choose a substitute from visual similarity.

What exactly does each image prove?

A complete-item image records visible shape, connectors and mounting features. A physical marking records what can actually be read on that item. A packaging label records the information printed on its packaging. Keep these observations separate: a correctly labelled box does not prove the identity of a different loose component.

How do I spot a mismatched photo pair?

Compare visible component type, connector arrangement, mounting points and other distinctive features across the photographs. If one image shows a pipe and another a sensor, flag the pair rather than selecting the clearer image. If both show similar parts but the full marking is unreadable, the identity remains unconfirmed.

A filename containing the requested OE is useful for organising files, but it is not independent identification evidence. Ask for an uncropped view showing the readable marking and enough of the surrounding component to connect the two. Keep the original file unchanged.

What should I ask the supplier to resend?

Quote your enquiry row identifier and the complete requested reference. Ask for a complete-item view, a legible physical marking where available, and a packaging label associated with that item. Describe which characters or features conflict. If there is no physical number, say so and request the catalogue basis for the proposed identification.

For Chinese-brand vehicle parts, include the known brand, model variant, production year and engine information with the request. These details support review; the sales model name alone does not establish application.

Can I accept the number on the replacement quotation?

Record a proposed alternative separately from the original reference. Ask the supplier to explain its identification basis before approving the change. Preserve every leading zero, letter, size and version segment. A shared prefix is not evidence of interchangeability.

A clear follow-up message

“Please keep our original reference unchanged. The supplied marking and photograph do not yet establish the same item. Resend a readable identification view tied to this enquiry line, or state what remains uncertain before quoting an alternative.”

Once identity is resolved, confirm quantity, unit, offered brand, currency, packing, availability and lead time for the actual quotation. This checklist supports purchasing review; it does not certify authenticity or fitment.
