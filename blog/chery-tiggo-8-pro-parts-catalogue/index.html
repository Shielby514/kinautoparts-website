Prepare a usable enquiry

Send one line per reference with the required quantity. Include photographs of the label, connectors and mounting points where relevant. Ask for written confirmation of specification and supply terms before ordering.

Prepare your quotation request

List the reference, vehicle variant, quantity and destination. Attach identifying photographs in your email or WhatsApp message. Confirm specification, lead time, packing, warranty and delivery terms in writing.
